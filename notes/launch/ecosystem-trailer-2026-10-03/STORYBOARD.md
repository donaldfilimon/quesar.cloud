# Build with understanding

90-second ecosystem orientation, with separately paced 30- and 15-second edits.
1920 × 1080, 30 fps. Original vector animation and original synthesized music.
No narration. Captions summarize the visual copy and are available as editable
JSON, SRT and WebVTT. This is a concept film, not an integrated system demo.

## Visual direction recorded before rendering

project. An editorial left column carries the name and idea. The right column
has a distinct moving construction; it is never framed as real product UI.

the status explicit. Product names are text wordmarks, not new official logos.

The 90-second product chapters each hold for twelve seconds. The 30-second cut
has a two-second opening, six four-second product chapters and a four-second
close. The 15-second cut has a one-second opening, six two-second chapters and
a two-second close. Both omit the separate convergence chapter. The shorter
cuts use role-sized copy and independently synthesized scores, preserving
natural music pitch and readable holds. They are not accelerated master files.

## Source-grounded copy and boundaries

`claims.json` pins the exact local source content hashes, Git revisions and line
locations consulted on 2026-10-03. A revision alone is insufficient in these
dirty shared checkouts; the file hashes identify what was actually read.

- **WDBX:** durable storage and typed episode ledger. Abstract record geometry
  illustrates that structure. No federation, GPU/OCR or retrieval-quality claim.
- **ABI:** orchestration and capability reporting. Moving pulses illustrate
  bounded runtime structure; they are not timings or a real execution trace.
- **Abbey:** the local CLI/TUI client. No claim of a separately complete desktop
  product, bundled model weights, or live provider acceptance.
- **Abbey Bot:** a separate Rust Discord service with commands and permission
  checks. Abstract channel blocks are not a captured Discord conversation.
- **Quesar / MLAI:** the current site as a view into products and research. The
  inspected Quesar route called it a large model, while the typed architecture
  catalog disclaimed a trained Quesar foundation model. This film uses only
  website-oriented copy and makes no trained-model claim. Quasar, spelled with
  an a, is the separate experimental builder and is not depicted here.
- **WebPress:** canonical WP-IR and its editing/code/export projections. No
  production hosting, universal plugin compatibility or full platform claim.

The WDBX local test-build screenshot was inspected as an optional visual. It is
not included: using abstract geometry consistently avoids giving one static
UI screenshot the meaning of executed inference. No existing media, screenshot
or font binary is incorporated into the film. The score and geometry are new.

## Acceptance contract

Before full capture: independently review this storyboard, claims, editable
source and representative previews. Validate every scene's text bounds and all
three caption schedules. After capture: check exact geometry/frame counts,
duration, H.264/yuv420p, 48 kHz stereo AAC, WAV sample counts, MP4 faststart,
full audiovisual decoding and measured loudness. Inspect decoded chapter
stills plus first/middle/final frames. Preserve all pre-existing media hashes.

Automated technical checks and sampled-frame review do not establish human
listening, real-time full-film review, deployed product behavior or a release.
