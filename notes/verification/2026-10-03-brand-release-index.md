## Brand use

Quesar by MLAI uses **Private AI operations.** Abbey/ABI own **Intelligence Without Limits**, bounded by their claims ledger. Keep that line out of Quesar’s hero and default OG. The [brand split](../mlai/brand.md) governs narrative; [site CSS](../../src/styles.css) and [cinematic token mappings](../../src/cinematic/design/mlai-ds-tokens.css) govern current appearance.

Display: Space Grotesk Variable. Body: IBM Plex Sans Variable. Code: IBM Plex Mono. Fonts already come from the repository’s @fontsource packages.

The [site mark component](../../src/components/site/logo.tsx) draws the weighted-graph M. The [favicon SVG](../../public/favicon.svg) is a distinct nested-square icon; it is not a vector master of the site mark. Older design-board tokens are reference material, not the token authority.
