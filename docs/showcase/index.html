  • Voice system

    On-device neural voice

    Narration is synthesized in your browser by the Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model (WebGPU, falling back to WASM), with each persona in its own voice and prosody. Nothing downloads until you press Play, and the text is never sent to a server. You can watch without the voice, and captions and a transcript carry the words either way.
