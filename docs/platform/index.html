Personas vs products

Two color axes. Do not mix them.

Product accents: WDBX cyan, ABI violet, Abbey emerald. Persona colors: Abbey emerald, Aviva violet, Abi cyan. The ABI product is violet; the Abi persona is cyan.

Abbey

Empathic polymath

High-EQ tutor and partner. Scaffolds, names uncertainty, and keeps the human in the loop.

Aviva

Unfiltered expert

Direct, concise, unhedged. Fewer tokens by design. Facts without preamble.

Abi

Adaptive moderator
